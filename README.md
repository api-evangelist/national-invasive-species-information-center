# National Invasive Species Information Center (national-invasive-species-information-center)

The National Invasive Species Information Center (NISIC) is the gateway to invasive species information covering federal, state, local, and international sources. Invasive species are plants, animals, or pathogens that are non-native to the ecosystem under consideration, and whose introduction causes or is likely to cause harm.
